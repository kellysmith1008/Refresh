# Introduction to CMS Basics: What is a CMS?
Welcome to the first course in our CMS Basics series, What is a CMS? This training teaches you what a content management system (CMS) is. At the VA, we use a CMS called Drupal to manage content for VA.gov. Let's take a walk through what a CMS is together to get us started!

## CMS Defined
A content management system (CMS) software that allows users to create, manage, and publish digital content (like websites or apps) without needing to write or edit custom code.
How it works: It provides a one-stop shop for storing content, such as apps, images, and websites, in a user-friendly interface that's customizable to an organization’s needs and employees.

____________________________________________________________________________

At the VA, we use Drupal as our CMS to manage important content for Veterans and their families. 
You may hear the term "decoupled Drupal" when disucssing the VA Drupal CMS. On the technical side, this means that you make changes in Drupal, but another system applies the style and formatting before Veterans see the content on VA.gov. But the easiest way to think about it is like this: you're the chef making the food in the kitchen, and then the food is handed to the server to deliver to the VA.

____________________________________________________________________________

We use Drupal CMS here at the VA because it provides:
* A single source of truth for information about VA benefits and health care through shared vocabulary and content libraries.
* A Veteran-centered template across organization type.
* An editor-centered approach to design that ensures you are empowered to serve our Veterans.
* An open-source code that can be customized and continuously improved
* A centralized location to house all images, videos, and content that allows easy and fast updating and editing.
* A system that the VA can support internally long term. 

## CMS Terms
As you go through your training, you will see a few key terms that we want to define for you before you get started. Don't worry, we'll continue to define these throughout the training, but here's your first introduction to them!

* __Content type__: A way to categorize content that is housed in a CMS. These can range from an image to an article, a page, or a video. On the technical side, a content type defines how different kinds of content relate to each other and how they're displayed on the site.
* __Section__: A way to decide which VA office or facility manages which content. This usually follows the VA organizational structure. On the technical side, this is the logic that helps determine, for example, that each VAMC is treated as its own section and is contained by VISNs and the Veterans Health Administration.
* __Node__: This is a term specific to Drupal but that you may see often in documentation. A node, simply put, is any piece of content within the CMS. This could be a page, an image, a block, an article, etc. On the technical side, nodes are how content is structured and displayed on a site.
