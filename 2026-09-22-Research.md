## Top of mind observations

**Observation #1**

- 3 out of 5 participants conveyed the course was helpful as an introduction to the CMS.
    - 2 participants mentioned the course was a good refresher of their onboarding information.
> "It was helpful to see how my role and other Drupal roles work."


**Observation #2**

- We need better messaging around course success.
    - 2 participants were unable to tell if they passed the knowledge exam at the end of the courses.
    - 3 participants passed the knowledge exam at the end of the courses.
    - Exam success was mixed among the courses.

**Observation #3**
- Both courses took under 30 minutes to complete
    - These courses, while introductory, were built with this completion timeframe in mind, so it was nice to see this assumption become a reality.  

**Observation #4**
- The courses prepared participants to use Drupal CMS.
    - 4 participants responded they might or could immediately apply what they learned in the courses.
    - One participant responded that they did not feel could immediately apply what they learned.
